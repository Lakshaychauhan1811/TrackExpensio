Setu Account Aggregator RBI Regulated
Account Aggregator is India's RBI-mandated open banking framework. All major Indian banks support it.

What is AA Framework?

Account Aggregator (AA) is an RBI-regulated fintech framework. Banks are legally required to share your data when YOU give consent. Your data flows securely via encrypted channels. Providers: Setu, Finvu, OneMoney, Perfios.

Setup Setu Sandbox (Free)

1. Visit setu.co/data
2. Sign up (sandbox free)
3. Get Client ID + Secret
4. Add to .env:
  SETU_CLIENT_ID=xxx
  SETU_CLIENT_SECRET=xxx
